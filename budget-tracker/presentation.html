BankingGo

A simple digital banking platform built to help everyone manage money clearly, safely, and confidently.

  • Built for literate and low-literacy users
  • Uses simple language and multilingual support
  • Includes an AI assistant called BankingGo
Slide 2

The problem

Many people cannot use banking apps because the language is confusing, the steps are hard to understand, and the design feels too advanced.

Pain point: Low literacy, language barriers, and fear of making mistakes stop people from using banking services.

Goal: Make banking easy for everyone, including communities that speak local or indigenous languages.

Slide 3

What BankingGo solves

  • Helps users open a simple bank account without complicated terms.
  • Supports multiple languages, including English, Kiswahili, Yoruba, and Luganda.
  • Can read the visible screen aloud when the device has a matching speech voice.
  • Tracks balance, budget, and spending in a clear and visual way.
  • Warns users early when they are overspending and explains what to do next.
Slide 4

Why it feels real

Banking UI: clean account cards, wallet activity, and budget visibility.

AI support: the BankingGo assistant explains savings, budgets, and overspending in plain language.

Inclusion: the app is designed for both literate and low-literacy users.

Trust: users can see how much they have, how much they spend, and how to protect their money.

Slide 5

Why I built it

  • I want to solve a real problem: many people are excluded from banking because of language and complexity.
  • This platform makes money decisions easier for families, market sellers, students, and everyday workers.
  • It is meant to help people save, stay safe, and feel confident about their money.
Slide 6

Security, ownership, and the path to launch

  • Today: this browser demo uses a salted, slow PIN hash, local storage, input checks, and a restrictive content policy.
  • Important: it is not tamper-proof, is not connected to a bank, and must not hold real customer or financial data.
  • Only four interface languages are translated in this prototype. More than 100 languages require reviewed translations, not just a longer selector.
  • Before launch: build server-side authentication, encryption, audit logs, monitoring, backups, accessibility and language testing, and independent security reviews.
  • Work with licensed banking/payment partners and meet each market's financial, privacy, consumer-protection, and accessibility rules.
  • Copyright notice: © 2026 BankingGo. All rights reserved. A notice states a claim; it does not prevent copying or hacking by itself.

This prototype's BankingGo helper uses local guided responses. A production AI assistant needs a protected service, privacy controls, and tested translations.
